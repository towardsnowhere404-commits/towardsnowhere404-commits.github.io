Nowhere Publisher

Uses YouTube API Services

Nowhere Publisher is a private, single-operator tool that uploads videos to YouTube channels owned and managed by its developer. It runs on the developer's own computer and is not offered to the public.

What it does

The developer places finished videos, together with their title, description, thumbnail and scheduled time, in a local folder. Nowhere Publisher uploads each video to the corresponding channel through the YouTube Data API, applies its metadata, and reads basic performance statistics (views, watch time, impressions) of those same channels to produce an internal weekly report.

Who can use it

Only the developer. Each channel is connected once through Google's standard OAuth consent screen, and access can be revoked at any time from the Google Account permissions page.
